## Campaign-level negative keywords

The following terms are intentionally added as **broad-match campaign negatives**. They target job-seeking, do-it-yourself research, equipment shopping/rental, repair work, and unrelated cleaning demand without blocking normal residential service requests.

| Theme | Negative keywords |
|---|---|
| Employment and training | `job`, `jobs`, `hiring`, `career`, `careers`, `salary`, `resume`, `indeed`, `apprentice`, `training`, `class`, `classes`, `course`, `certification`, `school` |
| DIY and research | `diy`, `how to`, `tutorial`, `tips`, `instructions`, `homemade` |
| Equipment and products | `rental`, `rent`, `rentals`, `equipment`, `machine`, `for sale`, `used`, `repair`, `parts`, `nozzle`, `pump`, `home depot`, `lowes`, `harbor freight` |
| Irrelevant vehicle/boat demand | `car wash`, `auto wash`, `truck wash`, `boat wash`, `rv wash` |
| Unoffered roof work | `roof repair`, `roof replacement`, `roof installation`, `roofing contractor` |
| Unrelated remediation | `mold remediation`, `water damage`, `fire damage`, `pest control` |

Google Ads supports campaign-level negative keywords, which lets a Search campaign exclude irrelevant queries before the ad enters the auction.[2]
