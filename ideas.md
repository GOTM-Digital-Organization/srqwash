# SRQ Wash — Design Brainstorm

## Response 1
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Industrial Precision meets Florida Coastal

**Core Principles:**
- High contrast dark navy + electric blue palette evoking power and water
- Bold asymmetric layouts with diagonal section cuts
- Photography-forward with dramatic before/after reveals
- Trust signals woven into every section (badges, ratings, certifications)

**Color Philosophy:**
Deep navy (#0A1628) as the dominant base — communicates professionalism and authority. Electric blue (#1E90FF) as the primary action color — water, power, energy. Warm amber (#F59E0B) as the conversion accent — draws the eye to CTAs without feeling aggressive. White text on dark backgrounds for maximum legibility.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Asymmetric split layouts — hero uses a 60/40 diagonal split with image on the right and headline on the left. Service cards use a staggered masonry grid. Section transitions use diagonal clip-paths (polygon cuts) rather than flat horizontal dividers.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Water droplet / pressure spray SVG motif used as section dividers and icon backgrounds
2. Diagonal stripe texture (subtle) overlaid on dark sections to suggest motion and power
3. Bold oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) as visual anchors

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Hover states reveal before/after sliders on service cards. CTA buttons have a water-ripple press effect. Scroll-triggered counter animations for stats (4.90 stars, 48 reviews, years in business).

**Animation:**
- Hero text slides in from left (180ms ease-out) with staggered word reveal
- Service cards scale from 0.96 on scroll entry (200ms)
- Diagonal section backgrounds parallax slightly on scroll
- CTA buttons: scale(0.97) on active, 160ms ease-out

**Typography System:**
- Display: Oswald Bold — strong, condensed, masculine — for H1/H2
- Body: Source Sans Pro Regular/SemiBold — clean, readable
- Accent: Oswald Regular for stat numbers and labels
</text>

**Core Principles:**
- Off-white / warm stone backgrounds with charcoal text — approachable authority
- Full-bleed photography with dark overlay gradients
- Generous whitespace as a signal of premium service
- Conversion-optimized with sticky CTA bar and floating call button

**Color Philosophy:**
Warm off-white (#F8F5F0) background signals cleanliness without clinical coldness. Deep charcoal (#1C2B3A) for text and structure. Sky blue (#3B82F6) for primary actions. Lime green (#84CC16) as a secondary trust accent (eco-friendly messaging).

**Layout Paradigm:**
Magazine-style editorial layout. Hero is full-bleed with a centered headline over a dramatic pressure washing action shot. Services section uses horizontal scroll cards on mobile, 3-column grid on desktop. Location pages use a sidebar layout with sticky quote form.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thick left-border accent lines on testimonial cards and section intros
2. Circular badge graphics for trust signals (Licensed, Insured, 5-Star)
3. Subtle grain/noise texture on hero overlays for depth

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Smooth scroll with section snap. Testimonial carousel auto-plays. Service cards expand on hover to show more detail. Phone number in header pulses subtly to draw attention.

**Animation:**
- Fade-up entrance for all content blocks (150ms stagger)
- Hero background has a slow Ken Burns zoom effect
- Counter animations for social proof numbers
- Sticky header compresses on scroll (logo shrinks, CTA stays visible)

**Typography System:**
- Display: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold — punchy, modern
- Body: Inter Regular/Medium — universal legibility
- Labels: Barlow SemiBold uppercase with letter-spacing

## Response 3 ← SELECTED
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Florida Power — Bold Brutalist Utility with Coastal Energy

**Core Principles:**
- Deep slate + pressure-wash blue + safety orange — powerful, trustworthy, action-oriented
- Oversized typography as a design element, not just text
- Asymmetric hero with truck/equipment photography bleeding to edge
- Every section ends with a conversion touchpoint

**Color Philosophy:**
Slate black (#111827) as the foundation — serious, premium. Pressure blue (#0EA5E9) as the primary brand color — water, sky, Florida. Safety orange (#F97316) exclusively for primary CTAs — maximum visibility, urgency without aggression. Light gray (#F1F5F9) for alternating section backgrounds. This palette is uncommon in the pressure washing space and will stand out.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Brutalist-inspired asymmetry: hero text is left-aligned and large, image occupies the right 45% with no padding. Service section uses a 2-column layout where odd services are left-image/right-text and even are reversed. Location pages use a full-width map background with overlay content card.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thick orange left-border accent on key callouts and quote blocks
2. Bold section labels in uppercase slate with a blue underline rule
3. Diagonal clip-path section transitions (top-right to bottom-left)

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Buttons feel physical — scale(0.96) on press, 140ms ease-out. Hover on service cards lifts with shadow. Sticky header with transparent-to-solid transition on scroll. Mobile: floating call button always visible.

**Animation:**
- Hero headline: word-by-word slide-in from bottom, 80ms stagger, 200ms ease-out
- Section content: fade-up from translateY(24px), triggered at 20% viewport entry
- Stats section: count-up animation on scroll entry
- Service cards: subtle scale(1.02) on hover with shadow deepening

**Typography System:**
- Display: Bebas Neue — ultra-condensed, bold, commanding — for H1/H2/H3
- Body: Nunito Sans Regular/SemiBold — warm, approachable, highly readable
- UI Labels: Nunito Sans Bold uppercase with 0.05em letter-spacing
</text>

## Selected: Response 3 — Florida Power

Rationale: The slate + pressure blue + safety orange palette is distinctive in the pressure washing market, the asymmetric layouts will make the site feel premium and hand-crafted, and the Bebas Neue / Nunito Sans pairing gives it authority without being generic.
